Choose which log levels produce popup notifications, while keeping every Warning, Error, and Fatal message available in history.

- Added independent Warning, Error, and Fatal checkboxes at the top of the history. All three are enabled by default.
- Unchecked levels remain recorded and can be viewed with F8. The choices apply to both live popups and startup summaries.
- Disabling a level removes its visible popups and startup-summary contributions. Re-enabling it applies to future messages without replaying old notifications.
- Choices persist across game restarts. Individual message mute settings remain independent. Reset all muted notifications enables every level and clears all individual mute settings.

**Upgrading from v0.1.3:** Existing configuration and individual mute settings are preserved. No manual settings changes are required. Close the game, replace the existing `BepInExLogAlerts` folder in `Sea Power_Data/StreamingAssets` with the folder from the ZIP, then restart the game. BepInEx and Anchor Chain are required.
